Group ID: S2_03 Description
Our group proposes a pinball game implemented on the Basys3
Group Theme:
FPGA board, combining arcade gameplay with real-time physics
FPGA Pinball Arcade
simulation. Players will control flippers to keep a ball in play, hit targets,
and achieve the highest possible score. The pinball table will appear on a
larger screen through the board’s VGA output and a VGA-to-HDMI
converter. Board buttons/mouse click will control the flippers and ball
launcher.
(Image for reference)

--- PAGE 2 ---
The game will incorporate gravity, acceleration, velocity, and energy
loss to produce realistic ball movement. Collision detection will determine
when the ball contacts walls, bumpers, targets, or moving flippers, while
bouncing logic will update its direction and speed. Additional features will
include randomized bonuses, animated effects, scoring, limited lives. We
will use separate hardware modules for physics calculations, collision
handling, game control, input processing, and graphics rendering. This
project will demonstrate how concurrent FPGA logic can coordinate
responsive controls, complex calculations, and interactive graphics.
